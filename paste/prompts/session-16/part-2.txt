## Case 17

Safety: No emergency room visits, police contact, or legal issues. Has not been threatened or attacked.
Family and social ties: Three grown children, in touch by phone at holidays. A grandchild who mails letters to a general-delivery address, and the letters are answered the same day.
A typical day: Breaks camp at first light so the site is not noticed, reads at the library until noon, then the drop-in center for a meal and a shower. Keeps to a strict schedule and looks forward to the library hours.
Goals: Senior housing. Is on two waiting lists and calls monthly to confirm the place on each.
Service history: Registered with the coordinated entry system fourteen months ago and has not been contacted since.
Presentation in the interview: Courteous and precise, with a mechanic's habit of explaining systems. Declines offers of help more than once before accepting.
