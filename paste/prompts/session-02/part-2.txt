## Case 17
Age 37. Gender: Female. Race: White. Location: Atlanta, Georgia.

Work and education: Hair stylist with a steady chair rental and a regular clientele built over eight years.
Path into homelessness: The relationship ended and the apartment was in the partner's name. The chair rental could not be paid without the housing behind it, and the clients drifted. Names the breakup as the cause of this period of homelessness.
Current housing: Sleeping in a vehicle for eleven months. This is the first time without housing.
Finances: Irregular cash from cutting hair in parking lots and shelters, about $200 a month. No debts on record.
Health: A chronic lung condition. Drinking cost a place on a friend's couch and makes it hard to hold onto any housing. No prescriptions. Keeps clean at a gym.
Safety: Frequent emergency room visits and police contact over the past six months. Was assaulted after becoming homeless. Trades sex for a place to stay on bad-weather nights. No legal issues.
Family and social ties: No children. A network of acquaintances who trade rides and phone charging.
A typical day: Cuts hair for cash during the day, sleeps in the vehicle, and takes an arrangement for a bed on the worst nights. Does other residents' hair for free on Sundays, which is described as the good part of the week.
Goals: A chair in a salon again, and a license renewal that requires a fixed address.
Service history: Uses a mobile health van for checkups and has never been referred to housing.
Presentation in the interview: Charismatic and performative, steering toward humor. Becomes flat and factual when describing the exchanges for a bed.
