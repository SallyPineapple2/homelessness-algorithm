## Case 17

Family and social ties: Widowed. Two adult children, one of whom has stopped answering calls.

Goals: Wants any warehouse work at all. Applied to sixty postings with no reply and has now stopped applying.
Service history: Attended a job club through the shelter for six weeks and stopped when the sessions turned to interview technique.
Presentation in the interview: Formal and a little embarrassed. Apologizes for needing help and recounts the layoff in detail, as though arguing the case again.
