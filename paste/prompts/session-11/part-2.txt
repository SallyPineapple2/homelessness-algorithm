## Case 17

Health: An untreated chronic respiratory condition makes stairs hard. Crowding and shared facilities have made regular bathing and clean clothes difficult. No prescriptions. Does not drink or use drugs.

Family and social ties: A younger sibling in foster care, visited monthly without fail. No children.
A typical day: Takes early delivery shifts, avoids the shelter's common areas since the assault, and sits outside with headphones until lights out. Describes nothing in the week beyond the shifts as fulfilling.
Goals: A room with a door that locks. Says this more than once, unprompted.
Service history: Reported the assault to shelter staff, who moved the bunk but filed nothing further.
Presentation in the interview: Jumpy and quick to apologize. Scans the room repeatedly and struggles to sit through the full interview.
