For every case, judge how vulnerable the person is, meaning how likely they are to suffer serious harm, including death, if they remain without housing.

Give each case a whole-number vulnerability score from 0 to 17, where 0 is the least vulnerable and 17 is the most vulnerable. Then rank all of the cases against each other by vulnerability: rank 1 is the person who should be prioritized first. Every case gets a different rank; break ties using your judgment. Base your judgment only on the information in the case summaries. For each case, give one sentence explaining the score.
