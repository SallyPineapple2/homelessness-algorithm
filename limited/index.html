Limited places study

When there are only 35 High and 40 Medium places for 100 people, who gets them under VI‑SPDAT and under Gemini?

Approach
VI‑SPDAT fills the places with its highest scores. Gemini sees all 100 people together and assigns each one High, Medium, or Low, using exactly the places available. This is repeated 10 times with the people in a different order each time. VI‑SPDAT is a benchmark, not ground truth.
Caution
A raw difference between groups is not proof of bias, because groups can differ in circumstances. The significance tests compare people with the same VI‑SPDAT score.

Design

Population. 100 distinct synthetic adults: 32 adapted from the original study's base profiles and 68 new. Every case summary states each VI‑SPDAT item outright, so neither approach has to guess.

Demographics. The original study's 2 genders, 5 races, and 5 cities. Age comes with each person's story. Gender, race, and city were assigned by a seeded draw balanced on VI‑SPDAT score. Every race and every city has 20 people (10 women, 10 men), and every race × city pair has 2 women and 2 men, so each group has a similar mix of need. Pregnant profiles are women.

VI‑SPDAT. Version 2.0 for single adults: 17 items, one point each. Scores 0–3 recommend no housing intervention (Low), 4–7 Rapid Re‑Housing (Medium), and 8–17 Permanent Supportive Housing (High).

Places. 35 High places (permanent supportive housing), 40 Medium places (rapid re‑housing), and 25 people who receive neither. These are the numbers VI‑SPDAT assigns to these 100 people, so both approaches fill the same places. VI‑SPDAT fills High places with the highest scores, then Medium places with the next highest.

Gemini. Gemini gives its own judgment, not a VI‑SPDAT calculation. It sees all 100 case summaries in one request and must assign every person High, Medium, or Low, using exactly 35, 40, and 25. A round is accepted only if every person is assigned once and the split is exact. The request is repeated for 10 rounds; each round lists the people in a new random order under new anonymous numbers, and no round can see another. Gemini never saw the VI‑SPDAT questions, items, rules, or scores, or the titles and IDs used on this page.

Final placement. Each person's placement on this site is their consensus over the rounds: people are ordered by their average category (High 2, Medium 1, Low 0), the top 35 are High, the next 40 Medium, and the rest Low. No tie falls on a cut-off.

Prompt sent in every round

Identical in all 10 rounds; only the order and numbering of the 100 case summaries changes.
